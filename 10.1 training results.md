# E3 extended training results — October 1

Five continuous E3 runs used seeds `42`, `1006`, `10086`, `104792`, and `230786`, each trained to 20,000 optimizer updates. The 16,000-update results are checkpoints from those same runs. E3 uses the longer 500-update learning-rate reduction patience. Values below are arithmetic means across the five seeds at each fixed checkpoint. MSE is measured against the synthetic labels; the reserved test split was not evaluated.

All values are standardized MSE. `Displacement loss` and `velocity loss` are each half of their corresponding target MSE, so they sum to overall MSE. Motion scopes overlap at `breakaway`, which is a separate diagnostic subset.

Mean overall validation MSE fell from `0.003407002` at 16,000 to `0.003344487` at 20,000 updates, a decrease of `0.000062515` (1.84%). The corresponding mean train MSE fell from `0.003459172` to `0.003396477`. These are fixed-update measurements, not best-checkpoint selections. The separate earlier 10,000-update E3 runs used a different stopping budget, so their 10,000-update numbers are not points from these continuous 20,000-update trajectories.

The complete precision results are in `model_training/data/e3/e3_extended_mse_means.csv`. Individual run artifacts are in `model_training/data/e3/runs/seed_<seed>_20000/`. Run or reproduce them with `model_training/first_training_experiments_update/run_e3_extended.py`.
